# PROJECT NAME

Pocket Pilot

---

# PROJECT VISION

Pocket Pilot is an AI-powered personal finance companion.

It is NOT another expense tracker.

The objective is to become a Financial Operating System.

The application should help users answer questions like:

* Can I afford this purchase?
* How much can I spend today?
* Will I achieve my savings goal?
* Where is my money leaking?
* What happens if my salary increases?
* Can I buy a new laptop this month?
* How much money will I have after 6 months?
* What is my financial health today?

The application should make financial decisions easier.
